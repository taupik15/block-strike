Audio

Weapon, radio, footsteps, grenade and interface sounds share the master volume. There is no background music.

Video / HUD

Shows FPS, frame time, position, input and last engine error. Errors also appear automatically during a match. No data is transmitted.

GoldSrc sprites load automatically from the bundled pack when available. An optional ZIP you have permission to use overrides it and is saved on this device.

    
    
    
30 FPS recommended for iPhone 13 and busy 32-player matches. 45/60 FPS are maximum targets, not guaranteed; higher rates may increase heat and battery use. The game lowers resolution if sustained frames are slow.

    
    
Graphics changes adjust render resolution and shadows. Screen resolution is managed by Safari and your device.

   

   
Voice Communication
Voice chat is unavailable in offline bot matches. Radio commands and their sound effects remain available during gameplay. No microphone permission is requested.

iPhone 13 landscape: 2/3/4-finger presets, draggable buttons and safe-area support. Touch controls can be customized during a match.
